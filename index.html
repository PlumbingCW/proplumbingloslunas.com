Water, pipes and the Rio Grande valley

The Village Water Division serves more than 6,000 water and sewer customers from the Santa Fe Group aquifer through four village wells. That groundwater leaves mineral scale on water heaters and fixtures, so a tank that runs for years without flushing tends to fail early.

Winter nights at this elevation can drop below freezing, which splits exposed pipes in garages and crawlspaces. Roots from cottonwoods along the bosque are the usual cause of repeat sewer clogs. Both problems are why a camera and a good diagnosis come before a repair.

Licensing and permits

New Mexico plumbing work follows the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the state Construction Industries Division issues plumbing permits. Contractor classifications include MM-1 for plumbing and MM-2 for gas. You can verify any contractor on the state license lookup before you hire.

Gas leaks are different. If you smell gas, leave the house, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside. A plumber comes after the area is safe.
