Standards behind every service

Every service on this site is done to the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) as adopted in New Mexico. That standard covers pipe materials, venting, fixtures, water heater safety devices and testing. Permits come from the state Construction Industries Division.

What every visit includes

Each visit follows the same sequence. A plumber arrives, looks at the problem and explains what they see. You hear a clear price before work starts. The repair is done and tested, and you get a plain explanation of what changed and what to watch.

We do not promise an arrival time we cannot control, and we do not quote prices over the phone for problems we have not seen. We tell you what we can and what we cannot.

Gas smells are different

If you smell gas, leave the building. Do not use a light switch, a phone or a garage door opener inside. From a safe distance, call the gas utility or 911. A plumber can inspect gas piping once the area is safe.

Gas appliances such as water heaters and ranges need licensed gas work in New Mexico. Contractor classifications include MM-2 for gas, and you can check any license on the state lookup page.
