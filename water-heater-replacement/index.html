Signs a failed tank has to be replaced now

A heater can fail slowly or all at once. The sudden failures are the ones that flood a closet or a garage. Water around the base, a tank that is warm to the touch on the outside or rust-colored water from the hot tap all point to a tank that has corroded through.

If the leak is from a fitting on top, a repair may do. If the leak comes from the tank body, no repair will hold. The plumber checks this first so you do not pay for a part on a dead tank.

Choosing the replacement: tank, tankless or heat pump

Most Los Lunas replacements are like for like, a new tank of the same size and fuel. That is the fastest path and the lowest upfront cost. A tankless unit saves floor space and can lower fuel use, but it needs the right gas line size, venting and sometimes an electrical upgrade. A heat pump water heater uses electricity very efficiently, though it needs room and airflow.

The tankless water heater page explains the tradeoffs in more detail, and the water heater installation page covers planned installs where you have time to compare.

Incentives you can ask about

The state of New Mexico offers a Sustainable Building Tax Credit for qualifying ENERGY STAR heat pump water heaters, administered by the Energy, Minerals and Natural Resources Department. The credit is a tax credit and not a rebate, and the amount depends on income and housing type. New Mexico Gas Company also lists increased rebates for high efficiency storage and tankless heaters.

Rules and amounts change, so check the programs before you buy and keep your paperwork. In an emergency swap you may not have time to apply first, so ask the plumber what documents to keep.

Permits and inspections on a replacement

Plumbing permits in Los Lunas are issued by the New Mexico Construction Industries Division, and the Village handles zoning and site items. The state plumbing code adopts the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code. Whether your replacement needs a permit depends on the scope, so ask when you book.

New Mexico licenses plumbing contractors under the MM-1 classification and journeyman plumbers under JP. You can look up any plumber’s license on the state license verification page.

What a water heater replacement costs depends on these five things

A larger tank or a tankless unit costs more than a standard 40 or 50 gallon tank.

Venting and gas line

Code and safety items

Expansion control, a drain pan, a relief valve line and seismic strapping where required.

Access

A tight closet or an attic install takes longer than a garage.

Before we arrive: failed or leaking tank

Small steps that cut damage and speed up the swap.

Shut off the cold supply

Turn the valve above the heater clockwise until it stops.

Cut power or gas

Switch off the breaker or set the gas control to off. Do not relight the pilot.

Clear a path

Move boxes and stored items so the old tank can be carried out.

Note the label

Photograph the data plate on the old heater. The fuel type, size and model help the plumber bring the right parts.

Permits and the Uniform Plumbing Code

A replacement water heater is normally a permitted job in New Mexico, and the work follows the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). That covers the relief valve, the discharge line, the gas or electrical connection and the venting on a gas unit. Ask your plumber who pulls the permit and when the inspection happens.
