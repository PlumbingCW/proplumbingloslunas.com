The village core, in documented facts

The Village of Los Lunas was incorporated in 1928 with 466 residents and counted 17,242 people in 2020. The comprehensive plan calls Main Street at NM 314 the oldest commercial area of the village. The Atchison, Topeka and Santa Fe Railroad arrived in 1880, and U.S. Highway 66 used this route in its first alignment in 1926, which is why the center sits where it does.

The Luna Mansion stands along Main Street and has been on the national and state historic registers since 1975. The Village of Los Lunas offices are at 660 Main St NW, and the Rail Runner station and Transportation Center anchor a transit-oriented area covered by a 2008 Station Area Plan.

We could not find a formal boundary for downtown, so this page treats it as the Main Street core and nearby streets. For anything on the edge, give your address and the dispatcher will confirm.

Why a mixed older core needs a different approach

A building in the village center may be a house, a shop or a house turned into an office. Each has different fixtures and a different owner, and tenants often cannot decide on repairs themselves. If you rent, tell the dispatcher who approves the work and who pays.

We do not know the pipe materials in any specific building here, and we do not assume that older means a particular defect. A plumber confirms the pipe type and condition on site, and a sewer camera inspection shows the line without digging.

Business owners and landlords

Restaurants and food businesses face grease in drains, and the village sets its own rules. We do not have the village’s grease requirements, so check with the village if you run a kitchen. A plumber can clear and jet the line, and your business is responsible for staying within the rules.

For a shop or office, ask for a written price that covers the fixture, the labor and any permit. If the work needs a permit, the state Construction Industries Division issues plumbing permits, and the plumber tells you whether yours does.
