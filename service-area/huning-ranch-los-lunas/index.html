Where Huning Ranch sits in Los Lunas

The Village of Los Lunas lists a Huning Ranch Area Plan among its planning documents, and the plan was last updated in December 2022. The area lies west of Interstate 25 and is reached from NM 6, which the village calls Main Street. The village comprehensive plan also names a large Huning Ranch business park, so the area mixes homes with future employment land.

Real estate sources describe the residential part as a master-planned community, often labeled West Los Lunas, with homes built from the early 2000s to the present. One housing guide lists a median year built of 2022, which fits a fast-growing area. Treat that figure as a listing-site estimate, because it is not an official count.

Anchors you can use when you call are Huning Ranch Park, Sundance Elementary School, the El Cerro de Los Lunas trailhead and Huning Ranch Loop. The park is a 12-acre neighborhood park according to the village Sierra Vista Area Plan.

Newer homes still need routine plumbing care

A house built in the last 20 years is not exempt from plumbing work. We do not know of any pattern of failures in Huning Ranch, and we will not suggest one. What we can say is general: water heaters collect sediment on any municipal supply, supply-line shutoffs under sinks go unused for years, and a toilet fill valve wears out on its own schedule.

Two habits help in any newer home. Learn where the main water shutoff is before you need it, and test the shutoff valves under each sink once a year so they turn when you need them. A valve that has never moved can seize, and that turns a small leak into a bigger one.

What we could not verify

We could not find an official boundary map that separates Huning Ranch from neighboring new-construction communities listed as West Los Lunas, so this page does not draw one. Several builder communities, such as Vista Sandia and The Trails at El Cerro, are marketed in the same part of town, and we cannot confirm which belong to Huning Ranch.

They overlap, but they are not identical. West Los Lunas is a label used by real estate sites, while Huning Ranch is a named master plan in the village planning documents. If you are unsure, give the dispatcher your street.

Give your street address, say what is happening and whether water is still running. If you can reach the main shutoff, turn it off first.

Inside the house the repair is between you and the plumber. If the work changes an exterior item, check your community rules. We do not have your association documents, so ask your association directly.

The village water utility serves the system up to the meter, and the pipe after the meter is the homeowner’s. Call the village for a problem at the meter and a plumber for a problem past it.
