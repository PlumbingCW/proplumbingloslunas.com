What Jubilee is, based on public sources

Jubilee at Los Lunas is a 55-plus community of single-family homes. A retirement-community listing counts about 400 homes, built between 2006 and 2024, first by Jenamar Communities and later by Avalon. The same listing describes a 12,000-square-foot clubhouse called The Villa, an outdoor pool and walking trails.

The Village of Los Lunas Sierra Vista Area Plan places the Jubilee subdivision along Jubilee Boulevard, south of NM 6 and next to Huning Ranch Park. Sundance Elementary School sits in the same part of town. A real estate guide describes the Jubilee homes as two-bedroom ranch-style houses, though plans range wider than that.

Because the community is gated, tell the dispatcher at the start of the call how a plumber gets in. A gate code or a call to the front gate saves time on the day of the appointment.

Plumbing jobs that suit a 55-plus household

Nothing about the age of a resident changes how pipes work. Preferences do change, and many homeowners in a community like this ask for comfort-height toilets, lever handles in place of round knobs and a shower valve that is easy to reach. Those are common requests, not a claim about Jubilee in particular.

Water heaters deserve a mention. A tank on a garage wall is easy to forget until it leaks, and the temperature setting matters for safety. Many manufacturers recommend a setting near 120 degrees, so check your unit’s label and ask the plumber if you want it adjusted.

  • Check under sinks and around the water heater for damp spots every few months.
  • Keep the path to the main shutoff clear so anyone in the house can reach it.
  • Ask a plumber before moving or capping any supply line.
  • Schedule non-urgent repairs for daytime hours when someone can be home to let the plumber in.
