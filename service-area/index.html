Towns we serve

Los Lunas itself uses the 87031 ZIP code. The towns below each have their own page, with local water and sewer details, common problems and a map.

Plumbing differs from place to place. Bosque Farms homes use village water and sit on larger lots. Peralta homes mostly use private wells. Belen has an older downtown and its own city wells. Rio Communities gets its water from a private utility. Knowing this saves time on the first call.

Los Lunas and the valley towns we reach

Our base is in Los Lunas, the Valencia County seat, which uses the 87031 ZIP code. The village has about 17,242 residents, and its Water Division serves more than 6,000 water and sewer customers from the Santa Fe Group aquifer. Plumbing there is mostly village water and sewer, from older homes to new subdivisions.

From that base, the same 24/7 line dispatches to four neighboring communities along the Rio Grande. Each has its own mix of water supply, sewer and housing age, which is why each has its own page.

Code and permits across the valley

The same 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC) applies in every town we serve, and permits come from the state Construction Industries Division. Zoning questions go to the local government.

What stays the same in every town

Whatever the town, the process is the same. One phone line, one dispatcher, a plumber who looks at the job first and a clear price before work starts. Emergency calls go to the front of the line.

What changes is the equipment and the questions. A plumber heading to a well home brings different gear than one heading to a village sewer address, and a downtown building needs different access than a subdivision house.

Why the difference matters when you call

A house on a private well has a pump and a pressure tank that a village house does not. A house on septic or a grinder pump behaves differently in a backup. A house in an older downtown building may have cast iron or clay pipe.

When the dispatcher knows which you have, the right plumber and the right tools arrive the first time. That saves you time and keeps the cost down.

Read the town page that matches your address: Bosque Farms, Peralta, Belen or Rio Communities.

A word on ZIP codes

ZIP codes help the dispatcher, but they do not follow town lines exactly. The 87002 code covers both Belen and Rio Communities, and the 87031 code covers Los Lunas. Mail and addresses can show a different city name than the town you live in.

When you call, give the street address and the city you live in. The dispatcher confirms the ZIP code on the town page and sends the plumber to the right door.

Outside these towns

If your address is in rural Valencia County but not in one of these towns, call anyway. Dispatch decides based on the address and the type of problem. Tell the dispatcher the nearest town and the road name.

We do not claim to cover places we cannot reach. If a job is too far, we say so on the call and do not waste your time.
