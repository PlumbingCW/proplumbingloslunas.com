How to reach us

Call (888) 910-8644 for any plumbing problem. If water is running, shut off the main valve first and then call. For a gas smell, leave the building and call the gas utility or 911.

You can also email contact@proplumbingloslunas.com for non-urgent questions. Email is not monitored for emergencies.

What to tell the dispatcher

  1. Your address and nearest cross street.
  2. What is happening and where in the house.
  3. Whether water is still running and the main is off.
  4. Whether the home uses village water, a private well, septic or a grinder pump.

This shortens the call and helps the plumber bring the right tools.

Before you call about permits

If your question is about a permit, the state Construction Industries Division issues plumbing permits under the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC). A plumber can tell you whether your job needs one.
