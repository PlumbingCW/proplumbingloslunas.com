Why the shower runs cold halfway through

Tankless heaters heat water as it flows. When the shower suddenly goes cold, three things are common. Mineral scale has narrowed the heat exchanger, the flow is below the minimum the unit needs to fire, or an error code has shut the burner down.

Scale builds up faster in some water than in others. The Village of Los Lunas pumps groundwater from the Santa Fe Group aquifer and publishes its quality report each year. The report tells you what is in the water, and your plumber can use it to set a flush schedule that fits.

Flush and descale service

A flush circulates a descaling solution through the heat exchanger to dissolve mineral buildup, then rinses it out. Most manufacturers recommend doing it yearly. If your unit has never been flushed and is more than a few years old, scale may already be restricting flow.

Without a flush, the unit works harder, fires less evenly and throws more error codes. With one, many units go back to normal. It is a quick service compared with replacement, and it is the first thing we check on a malfunctioning tankless unit.

Installing a tankless unit properly

A tankless install is more than a swap. The gas line often needs to be larger than a tank heater uses, because the unit burns a lot of fuel in a short time. Venting is specific to the model, and the condensate from high-efficiency units needs a drain path.

Plan for it on the water heater installation page, where permits, code and licensing are covered. If you are choosing between tank and tankless because a tank just failed, emergency replacement covers that decision under pressure.

Is a tankless heater right for your home?

Tankless suits households that run out of hot water, homes with limited floor space and people who want to avoid standby heat loss. It does not suit every house. If the existing gas line is far too small or there is no practical vent route, the added work can outweigh the benefit.

It also does not make water hotter or fix low water pressure. If your problem is a cold shower at the farthest bathroom, a recirculation loop or a second unit may do more than a bigger heater.

Heat pump or electric options

Electric tankless units draw a lot of current at once and often need a dedicated high amperage circuit. For homes without gas, a heat pump tank can be a lower operating cost choice, and the state tax credit for ENERGY STAR heat pump water heaters may apply. Check the current amounts with the Energy, Minerals and Natural Resources Department before you buy.

Gas, venting and the UPC

Tankless units draw a large gas load and need correct venting. New Mexico plumbing work follows the 2021 Uniform Plumbing Code (UPC), and the state Construction Industries Division issues the permit. Ask the installer to show the gas line size and the vent material on the quote.
