Stop the water first

Find the main shutoff, usually where the water line enters the house near the street side or in a garage or utility closet. Turn it clockwise until it stops. Then open a faucet at the lowest point of the house to drain the remaining pressure.

If you cannot find the valve or it will not turn, call right away. A plumber can close the line at the meter or the curb stop.

Why pipes burst in the Rio Grande valley

The most familiar cause is freezing. Los Lunas sits at about 4,860 feet, and clear winter nights can fall well below freezing. Water in an exposed pipe turns to ice and expands, and the split often shows only when the ice thaws.

Other causes include corrosion in older metal pipe, high pressure, a failed joint and ground movement. A pipe that bursts in a warm month points to age or pressure and not weather.

Frozen but not yet burst

If a pipe is frozen and no water is coming out, open the faucet and warm the pipe slowly with a hair dryer. Never use a flame. Start near the faucet and work back toward the frozen section.

If you see a split or a bulge, do not thaw it. Shut off the main and call. For the follow-up, read about water line repair if the break is outside the house.

After the repair: drying and documenting

Once the leak is stopped, the next job is drying. Wet drywall and insulation can grow mold in a couple of days. Take photos of everything before you move it, and keep receipts for insurance.

If you are not sure where the water went, a leak search can find hidden moisture. Our leak detection page describes how that works. A water damage restoration company handles drying, and we can point you to the right step.

Pipe materials in older and newer homes

Older houses in Los Lunas, Belen and Bosque Farms may have galvanized steel or copper. Newer homes often have PEX or CPVC. Each fails differently. Steel corrodes from the inside, copper can pinhole, and plastic pipe can split at fittings or when frozen.

If your home has had several leaks, the piping may be reaching the end of its life. Repiping replaces the system and ends repeat repairs.
